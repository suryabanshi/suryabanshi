# Document checklists by procedure

> Starting templates. Always confirm the *current* required-documents list for the specific
> status on the Immigration Services Agency (出入国在留管理庁) website — lists differ by status,
> employer category, and applicant circumstances.

## Common to most applications
- Application form (the correct form for the procedure), completed and signed.
- Passport + current 在留カード (residence card), if in Japan.
- One recent photo per current spec (size, background, recency).
- Return envelope / correct fee in 収入印紙 (revenue stamps) where required.

## COE for a work status (在留資格認定証明書 — sponsor files in Japan)
Applicant provides:
- Passport copy; academic transcripts/diploma or proof of relevant experience; CV.
Sponsor (employer) provides:
- Employment contract or offer; company registration (登記事項証明書); recent financial statements;
  company overview; the reason/explanation letter tying the role to the applicant's background.

## Extension (在留期間更新)
- Application form; passport + 在留カード; photo.
- Proof the activity continues (employment certificate + recent withholding slip 源泉徴収票, or
  enrollment certificate for students); tax and residence-tax payment certificates
  (納税証明書 / 課税証明書) where requested.

## Change of status (在留資格変更)
- Application form; passport + 在留カード; photo.
- Full documentation for the **new** activity (as in the COE work list) — treat it like a fresh
  eligibility case for the target status.

## Family — Dependent (家族滞在) / Spouse of Japanese or PR
- COE application form; sponsor's proof of status and income (employment certificate, tax
  certificates, resident record 住民票 showing household).
- Proof of relationship: **marriage certificate** (spouse) / **birth certificate** (child),
  officially translated into Japanese; sometimes photos/correspondence for spouse cases.
- A clear reason letter describing the relationship and how the family will be supported.

## Permanent residence (永住許可)
- Application form; reason letter; long-run proof of income, tax, and pension/insurance payment;
  guarantor documents; resident record; documents showing length and stability of residence.

## Notes on foreign documents
- Foreign civil documents usually need a **certified Japanese translation**; some require
  **apostille or consular legalization**. Confirm which per the applicant's country.
