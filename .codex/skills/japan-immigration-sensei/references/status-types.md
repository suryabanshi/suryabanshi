# Japan Residence Statuses (在留資格) — quick reference

> Reference only. Conditions and eligibility change — verify against the Immigration Services
> Agency of Japan (出入国在留管理庁) before advising on a specific case.

## Work statuses (就労資格)

| Status (日本語 / English) | Fits | Key conditions |
|---|---|---|
| 技術・人文知識・国際業務 / Engineer · Specialist in Humanities · International Services | Most white-collar/technical jobs | Degree or relevant experience matching the role; contract with a Japanese employer; job must match field |
| 高度専門職 / Highly Skilled Professional (i, ii) | High-scoring professionals | Points-based (education, income, career); fast-track to permanent residence |
| 特定技能 / Specified Skilled Worker (SSW i, ii) | Designated labor-shortage sectors | Skills + Japanese-language tests, or transition from 技能実習; SSW ii allows family and longer stays |
| 技能実習 / Technical Intern Training | Trainees via accredited programs | Through a supervising organization; strict program rules |
| 経営・管理 / Business Manager | Founders/executives of a business in Japan | Real business, office, capital/scale requirements |
| 企業内転勤 / Intra-company Transferee | Transfer within the same company group | Prior employment at the overseas office |
| 技能 / Skilled Labor | Specialized manual skills (e.g., certain chefs) | Documented years of experience |
| 教育・教授・研究・医療・法律・会計 等 / Instructor, Professor, Researcher, Medical, Legal, Accounting, etc. | Regulated professions | Profession-specific qualifications |

## Family / relationship statuses (身分・地位に基づく資格)

| Status | Fits | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 家族滞在 / Dependent | Spouse & children of a worker/student resident | Work only with permission (資格外活動許可), usually capped hours |
| 日本人の配偶者等 / Spouse or Child of Japanese National | Married to / child of a Japanese national | No work restriction |
| 永住者の配偶者等 / Spouse or Child of Permanent Resident | Family of a PR holder | No work restriction |
| 定住者 / Long-Term Resident | Certain other family/humanitarian situations | Conditions vary; often no work restriction |
| 永住者 / Permanent Resident | Long-settled residents | Separate 永住許可申請; strict income/tax/residence-length criteria |

## Study / other

| Status | Fits |
|---|---|
| 留学 / Student | Enrolled students; part-time work only with 資格外活動許可 |
| 特定活動 / Designated Activities | Job-seeking graduates, some dependents, working holiday, and other special cases |
| 短期滞在 / Temporary Visitor | Tourism/business ≤ 90 days; not a work permit |

## Choosing between routes

- **Long-term work/life →** identify the matching work status and use the **COE** route.
- **Joining a resident family member →** Dependent (家族滞在) or the appropriate spouse/child status.
- **Already in Japan, situation changed →** Extension (same status) or Change of status.
- **Short visit →** visa exemption or **JAPAN eVISA**; never for working.
